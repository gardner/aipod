Welcome to AI Pod! In this introductory episode, we explain how we use AI to transform complex government reports into accessible audio content.

## What We Do

AI Pod takes large hundred+ page reports published by the government and turns them into AI generated podcasts. Our mission is to make complex policy documents more accessible and understandable for everyone.

## How It Works

We analyze official government publications, extract key insights, and generate natural-sounding audio summaries that capture the essential information without the jargon.

Starting with our next episode, we'll dive into specific government reports, breaking down important policies and documents that affect your daily life.
